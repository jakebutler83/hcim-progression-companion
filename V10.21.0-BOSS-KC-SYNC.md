# v10.21.0 — Boss KC sync

Account Sync now reads supported boss completion counts from the same official hiscore response already used for clue totals. Published counts are sent as `bossKillCounts` in the private account snapshot.

Combined activities use a total across their official variants:

- Dagannoth Kings: Prime + Rex + Supreme
- Chambers of Xeric: normal + Challenge Mode
- Theatre of Blood: normal + Hard Mode
- Tombs of Amascut: normal + Expert Mode

Unranked and unsupported activities are omitted instead of being reported as zero, allowing the website to request a manual KC without displaying misleading data.
